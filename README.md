## Elijah Purcell

Elijah Purcell is the Founder/CEO of Purcell Ventures LLC, a Georgia software
company that builds AI systems and custom software for small businesses and advises owners
on running them. He studies psychology and data science in New College and the Honors College
at the University of Alabama, Class of 2030, on a pre-med track toward psychiatry, and
works as an independent contractor with Mercor Intelligence on AI model training projects.

He founded the company at seventeen, in April 2025, and runs it from Tuscaloosa, Alabama.

### What I am working on

Psychology and data science at Alabama, aimed at psychiatry, with a specific interest in
how AI can reduce the administrative load carried by mental health clinicians. Most of
what is in this account is the same question from the software side: what a system can
take over, and what has to stay with a person.

### Free tools, live right now

Most of the repositories here are single-file web tools that run in your browser, store
what you enter on your own device, and ask for nothing. A cipher workbench, a decision
journal that scores your own predictions, a tiny CRM for the people you actually care
about, an Ignatian examen, a printable day sheet, a scripture memoriser.

They are all live at `thebigjah.github.io/<repo>/`, and there is an index at
[purcellventures.co/tools](https://purcellventures.co/tools).
